Essential Starter Kit

You do not need everything. Start with a small, reliable set and upgrade later.

  • Sharp knife or craft knife
  • Metal ruler
  • Cutting mat
  • Stitching chisels
  • Harness needles
  • Waxed thread
  • Contact adhesive
  • Edge beveler
  • Burnisher

Tool guide

Beginner Tools

Prioritised from essential tools to useful upgrades.

ToolUsePriorityBeginner Tip
Sharp knife / craft knifeCutting leather cleanlyEssentialSharp blades matter more than expensive handles
Metal rulerStraight cuts and measuringEssentialUse metal, not plastic, when cutting
Cutting matProtecting your table and bladeEssentialUse a self-healing mat if possible
Stitching chiselsPunching even stitch holesEssentialStart with 3.85 mm or 4 mm spacing
Needles and waxed threadHand stitchingEssentialUse blunt harness needles, not sharp fabric needles
Edge bevelerRounding sharp leather edgesUsefulStart with size 0 or 1 for small goods
BurnisherSmoothing and polishing edgesUsefulWood or canvas both work well
Contact adhesiveHolding layers before stitchingUsefulApply thinly and let it go tacky first
Scratch awlMarking lines and holesUsefulMark lightly so mistakes are not permanent
Wing dividerMarking stitch linesUpgradeHelpful once you make cleaner finished goods

Best starter setup

For your first project, buy a knife, metal ruler, cutting mat, stitching chisels, two harness needles, waxed thread and a small piece of 3–4 oz vegetable-tanned leather.
