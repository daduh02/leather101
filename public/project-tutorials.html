Start here

These projects are chosen because they use small pieces of leather, simple cuts and basic saddle stitching. Start with the key fob or bookmark if you have never made anything before.

Project 1

Simple Cardholder

Best leather: 3–4 oz vegetable-tanned leather.

Tools: knife, ruler, stitching chisels, needles, waxed thread, edge beveler and burnisher.

  1. Cut one back panel and two pocket panels.
  2. Bevel and burnish the top edge of each pocket first.
  3. Glue the pockets lightly onto the back panel.
  4. Punch stitching holes around the sides and bottom.
  5. Saddle stitch, backstitch, trim and finish the edges.

Project 2

Key Fob

Best leather: 4–5 oz vegetable-tanned leather.

Difficulty: Very easy.

  1. Cut a strip of leather around 20 mm wide.
  2. Round the ends with a coin or corner template.
  3. Punch a hole for the key ring or rivet.
  4. Bevel, sand and burnish the edges.
  5. Add a key ring, rivet or Chicago screw.

Project 3

Leather Bookmark

Best leather: 2–3 oz or 3–4 oz vegetable-tanned leather.

Difficulty: Very easy and good for practice cuts.

  1. Cut a long rectangle or pointed bookmark shape.
  2. Punch a small hole at the top for cord if wanted.
  3. Add initials, stamping or a simple crease line.
  4. Sand and burnish the edges.
  5. Apply a light leather balm or finish.

Project 4

Leather Coaster

Best leather: 4–5 oz vegetable-tanned leather.

Difficulty: Easy.

  1. Cut a circle or square coaster shape.
  2. Round or bevel the edges.
  3. Add a decorative stitch line or stamped border.
  4. Burnish the edges smoothly.
  5. Seal the top surface to help resist marks.

Recommended order

  1. Bookmark — easiest cutting and finishing practice.
  2. Key fob — introduces holes, hardware and edge finishing.
  3. Coaster — good for curves, stamping and finishing.
  4. Cardholder — introduces layers, gluing and saddle stitching.
